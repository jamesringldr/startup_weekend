# Event Guide — Techstars Startup Weekend KU

**What:** Techstars Startup Weekend, University of Kansas — first-ever KU edition.
**When:** November 13–15, 2026.
**Where:** Capitol Federal Hall, Lawrence, KS.
**Focus areas:** FinTech (named), AI, DeepTech. Open to KU alumni.
**Dress:** casual. **Bring:** laptop (especially Day 2 workshops), notebooks, business cards, charger. Meals provided all 3 days, plus a closing dinner Sunday.

## Logistics and eligibility
- **Check in at the registration desk all 3 days.** This is a hard deliverable, not a nicety.
- **Every participant present in person on a team Day 1–3 is eligible for a $250 credit for token builds** after the event.
- **Every full 3-day participant may submit an idea after the weekend to KU staff, considered for the KU mentorship program and Techstars Global Founder Catalyst — explicitly NOT dependent on winning.** Open question (ask Bowen or Eromosele early): must the submitted idea be the weekend idea, or can it be any idea (i.e., can it carry Vanyshr)?
- **How to prepare (from the event's own page):** research the problem, practice your 60-second pitch, get familiar with the Lean Canvas, **get enough rest before the event.**

## Friday Day 1 — pitching and team formation
- **Register your pitch idea at the registration desk.**
- **60 seconds, on the event's template. You must share a name for the idea/startup.** No slides Friday. Props OK. One idea only.
- **The template (verbatim shape):** "Hi, I'm [Name], founder of [Startup Name]. Today, [target audience] struggle with [specific problem]. This leads to [negative impact]. Our idea is to develop [product or service] that will help [target audience] [main benefit]. To do this, we'll need [team skills]."
- **Pre-started ideas are allowed** if seeking validation. **An incorporated business with customers is NOT pitchable** (one more reason Vanyshr was never a Friday candidate).
- **If your idea isn't selected, you may still work on it ONLY if you have more than 3 other people on the team (team of 4+), subject to a 15-team cap.**

## Sunday — the final pitch
- **3 minutes total per team, hard stop, + 2 minutes Q&A** (another event page says "2 min judges feedback" — treat it as one 2-minute Q&A/feedback window).
- **Slides must be Google Slides.** Live demos must exist **IN the slide**. Approved formats include product videos → **primary plan: recorded demo video embedded in the Slides deck; live demo is a stretch goal.**
- **Each team shares ONE Google Slides link on Day 2.** Don't build in Figma/Canva/PowerPoint and convert late.
- **Sunday: Tech Check by 1:00pm; at 4:30pm slides are pulled into the master deck and cannot be edited after.**
- **The Lean Canvas (.pptx provided) is the official framework.**

## Judging criteria — the official rubric (five criteria, each scored 1–5)
Benchmarks are described at 1 / 3 / 5 in the event's rubric.

1. **Customer Validation** — target customers/problem; what you learned from talking to potential customers and/or data research; compare vs existing alternatives (better/faster/cheaper/more effective). Strongest = compelling, distinctive solution with clear competitive advantage.
2. **Team Dynamic** — complementary skills; **share presentation responsibilities across the team**; everyone engaged and ready for Q&A. Highest rating when all members share delivery evenly. **A score of 1 is literally defined as "delivery by one person"** — a solo-delivered Sunday pitch floors this criterion.
3. **Business Model** — sustainable business; who pays, what for, revenue streams. Strongest = clear sustainable model with **multiple revenue streams**.
4. **Execution and Design** — clear, complete, well-prepared pitch; show what you built and how customer feedback shaped it; demos/MVPs; prototype in any medium.
5. **Impact** — positive difference; who benefits (people/society/environment); improves on existing solutions; why benefits matter and how widely they reach. Strongest = significant positive impact, wide-ranging benefits.

**"Preparing your pitch" page:** cover all five criteria, use depictions of prototype/MVP, support claims with examples, explain revenue model and intended impact, **rehearse together so everyone knows how they will contribute.**
**"What should be accomplished by Sunday" page:** validated with real users; built a functional MVP; have a future plan.

## Mentorship mechanics
Two formats.

**Guest Mentorship: Day 2 (Saturday), 2:30–5:00pm**, dedicated area. Mentorship schedule/room designations on Notion by start of Day 2. **Each team gets exactly TWO 30-minute 1:1 sessions with different mentors — pairings appear ASSIGNED (team/mentor/room labels are placeholders until confirmed on Day 2), not chosen.**

**In-house Team Office Hours: Day 2 & 3, 7:00–8:00pm & 11:00am–12:00pm** — teams move between mentors; some mentors circulate for quick check-ins; sessions 20–30 min.

**Session protocol:** introduce yourself/team member/startup name; explain idea **max 5 minutes**; share what you learned from potential customers; compare vs existing alternatives; ask advice on 1 or more challenge areas; gather feedback; ask about gaps; **always ask: "who else do you think I should be talking to about this idea?"** Bring a teammate to sessions.
