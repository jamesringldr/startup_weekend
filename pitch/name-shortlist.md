# Name Shortlist — UNPICKED

None of these is trademark- or domain-checked; that check comes after James picks. All are short enough to say in the pitch's first line ("I'm James Oehring, founder of [NAME]").

1. **Strike** — options strike price + a sports strike; shortest, most memorable.
2. **HedgeLine** — hedging + the betting line; says what it does.
3. **OpenBook** — the open bid/ask book; transparency as the point.
4. **FairBook** — echoes the Betfair proof point, "a fair market."
5. **SecondHalf** — you can adjust mid-game instead of being locked in; the most evocative, least descriptive.
