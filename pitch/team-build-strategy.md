# Team Build Strategy — Exchange Gets Picked

Source: the official formats and rubric from the event's live Notion pages (Friday 60-sec template pitch, Sunday 3-min team-shared pitch + 2-min Q&A, five criteria scored 1–5, Google Slides link Day 2, slides freeze Sun 4:30pm, final pitches 6:30pm) and the Judges/Mentors Recon dossiers. James's persona direction: **"the operator who ships"** — plain, specific, fast; numbers when he has them, "I don't know yet" when he doesn't.

The shape of the team is the shape of the Sunday pitch. Five people, five jobs.

## Seat 1 — James: Founder / market architect / closer
- **Owns:** vision, market-structure design, team orchestration (intake, checkpoints, mentor sessions), regulation + market-math answers in Q&A (where Fein and Clarke will come at you).
- **Sunday:** opens and closes the pitch.
- **Does NOT do:** build the demo himself, or present the whole three minutes alone. The second one literally scores a 1 on Team Dynamic (defined as "delivery by one person").

## Seat 2 — Builder: the demo
- **Owns:** the working product. Scope is deliberately narrow — one league, one contract type, live bid/ask book, place a position, watch the price move, exit the position. Records the demo video that gets embedded in the slide.
- **Sunday:** presents the demo segment (~35 sec).
- **Recruiting test (Friday night):** "What's the last thing you shipped in a weekend?" A repo or a link = real. An idea = not your builder.

## Seat 3 — Validation lead: the evidence
- **Owns:** the Saturday interview sprint — real bettors, the "wanted out of a position and couldn't exit" story, tallied into numbers with honest denominators. Criterion #1 (Customer Validation) is won or lost here.
- **Sunday:** delivers the validation segment (~35 sec) — the numbers land harder from the person who collected them, and it's your second voice on stage.
- **Recruiting test:** "Have you ever interviewed a stranger about a problem?" Watch whether they ask a follow-up question or just pitch you their own idea.

## Seat 4 — Numbers / business model: the math that foots
- **Owns:** market sizing that actually foots (no unfooted TAM — Fein kills that in Q&A), revenue streams (trading fees on matched trades, market data/API, premium tools — the rubric rewards plural streams), unit economics (what the spread pays for, take rate, liquidity cost). Also owns the competitor scan: DraftKings, Betfair, Kalshi/Polymarket — why the exchange is different, why the US regulatory path is staged, not dodged.
- **Sunday:** presents the business-model segment (~35 sec); owns the Q&A math.
- **Who to look for:** a finance or econ student — the room is full of them.

## Seat 5 — Design / slides owner: the artifact
- **Owns:** the Google Slides deck end-to-end (link shared Day 2, frozen Sun 4:30pm), the Lean Canvas visual, the recorded demo video edit. The deck IS the pitch; Execution & Design is a scored criterion.
- **Who to look for:** not "the person who's good at Canva" — the person who understands the deck is the deliverable.
- **If only four people:** this seat merges with the builder (a builder who can make things look clean) or the validation lead. It cannot be James — he'll be running the room.

## Sunday mapping (3:00, team relay)
James opens — problem (~30s) → Validation lead — evidence (~35s) → Builder — demo (~35s) → Numbers — model (~35s) → James closes — impact + staged path (~30s). Q&A pre-assigned by topic: regulation and market math to James, validation follow-ups to the validation lead, liquidity to the builder.

## Friday-night recruiting notes
- **The dinner hour (5–6pm, before pitches) is where you pre-sell** — have the five seats in your head before you walk in.
- **Red flags:** anyone who wants to debate the idea for an hour instead of building it; anyone who can't commit all three days (check-in and the post-weekend submission both require full attendance); anyone who says "I'll just help where needed" — a person with no seat is a team that scores low on Team Dynamic.
- **Middle branch if not selected by vote:** you can still run your own idea with a self-formed team of 4+ (15-team cap). Same five seats, same plan.
