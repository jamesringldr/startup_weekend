## The script (~145 words, ~60 seconds spoken)
> I'm James Oehring, founder of [NAME].
>
> If you've ever bet on a game, you know the problem: the second you place it, you're locked in. The line moves against you, your quarterback gets hurt, and there's nothing you can do but watch it lose.
>
> That lock-in costs bettors billions — and it's the one thing sports betting never fixed, even as it went legal in most of the country.
>
> [NAME] is an options exchange for sports. Calls, puts, and spreads on games, with a live bid and ask — so you can enter a position, and exit it, the way you'd trade a stock. Settle in USDC, so payouts are instant and global.
>
> Betfair proved the model works everywhere else in the world. They just block the US. This is the American exchange for it.
>
> I need a builder for the live demo, someone to run validation interviews Saturday, a numbers person for the model, and a designer for the deck. Find me at dinner — let's build it.

## Team ask, mapped to the seats already planned
- Builder: demo = one league, one contract type, live bid/ask book, place and exit a position.
- Validation lead: Saturday interview sprint.
- Numbers / business-model owner: market sizing, plural revenue streams, unit economics.
