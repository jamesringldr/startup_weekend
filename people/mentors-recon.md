## Mentorship mechanics (from the event pages)
- **Guest 1:1s: Saturday 2:30–5:00pm. Each team gets exactly TWO 30-minute sessions with different mentors — pairings appear ASSIGNED, not chosen.** If any influence over assignment exists when the schedule posts Saturday morning, asks are **Wagner** (market design) and **Morris** (evidence / January audition).
